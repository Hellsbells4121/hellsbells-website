How it works

Ordering in three steps

1. Get in touch

Tap Order on a piece, or message me on stream. Tell me which one you would like and where it is going.

2. I send an invoice

You get a clear invoice with the price and how to pay. Bank transfer works, and I can arrange Twitch gift subs if you would rather pay that way.

3. I make it and post it

Once payment arrives, your piece is packed and sent. I will let you know when it is on its way.

Want something made just for you?

Custom pieces are taken case by case. Tell me what you are picturing and I will let you know what is possible.
